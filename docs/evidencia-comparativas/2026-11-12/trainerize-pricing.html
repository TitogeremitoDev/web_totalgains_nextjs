Yes. Trainerize offers a permanent free plan that allows you to coach one client at no cost, with no credit card required. The free plan includes the mobile app, workout programming, basic nutrition tracking, and in-app messaging and never expires. Trainerize is one of only two platforms in this category (alongside Everfit) to offer a permanent free tier.

Trainerize pricing starts at $10/month for the Grow plan (up to 2 clients). The Pro plan starts at $25/month for up to 5 clients, scaling to $135/month for up to 50 clients. The Studio Plus plan for fitness businesses starts at $275/month for up to 500 members. Annual billing saves 10% across all paid plans. All prices are in USD.

Coaching clients have full access to training programs, workout tracking, meal tracking, habit coaching, and in-app messaging. Basic clients can book appointments and make purchases through the app, but cannot access training programs, track workouts or meals, or use in-app messaging. Basic clients do not count against your plan's client limit, which means you can have an unlimited number of Basic clients on any paid plan.

At the end of your trial, your account automatically reverts to the free plan (1 coaching client, no credit card charged). You do not need to cancel. If you want to continue coaching more clients, you can upgrade to any paid plan at any time, before or after the trial ends. Add-ons are not included in the trial period.

Trainerize offers four paid add-ons for Grow and Pro plan users: Advanced Nutrition Coaching ($20/month on Grow/Pro 5/Pro 15; $45/month on Pro 30 to Pro 200), Video Coaching ($10/month on Pro plans, includes 50 hours of video calling and 100 hours of streaming), Business Add-on ($25/month, includes scheduling, referrals, payments, and on-demand coaching), and Stripe Integrated Payments ($10/month). The Pro Custom Branded App is available as a one-time $169 setup fee. All add-ons plus a Studio Custom Branded App are included at no extra cost on Studio and Enterprise plans.

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your Trainerize plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades to a smaller plan apply at the end of your current billing cycle. Trainerize does not issue refunds for unused portions of a subscription. There are no long-term contracts — all plans are month-to-month unless you choose annual billing.

When changing to a larger plan or a longer billing cycle, the change is applied immediately and the pro-rated difference is charged to your account.

When changing to a smaller or Free plan, or when switching to a shorter billing cycle, the change is applied at the end of the current billing cycle. You can continue to use your current paid plan until that date. We are unable to issue refunds for unused portions of your subscription.

If you have a Custom Branded App, it will be deactivated and you will need to use the main ABC Trainerize app.
